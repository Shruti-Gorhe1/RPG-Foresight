Fail-closed controls

Stale feed, missing source attribution, absent approval, invalid recipient, policy outage or audit failure means no notification.

Incident response

Pause delivery, preserve evidence, notify the accountable security/privacy/legal owners, and resume only after documented clearance.

Responsible global deployment

Private signals first; separate controls

The primary purpose is to route approved signals from each customer's own bid pipeline to named subsidiary recipients. Global public-tender discovery is a separate secondary capability; neither flow bypasses its own source and permission controls.

1
Primary private-signal authorizationRead only approved pipeline fields; require a current decision for sender, receiver entity, named user, purpose, fields, channel and expiry.
2
Secondary public-source integrityUse permitted official interfaces; retain attribution, lifecycle status, freshness and source link.
3
Separate storage and accessTenant-scope preferences and private group data; public source processing must never bypass private authorization.
